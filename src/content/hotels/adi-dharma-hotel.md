Adi Dharma Cottages is situated right at the heart of Kuta - Bali on bustling Legian street where wide selection of shop, cafe, entertainment and dinning are within easy walking distance. has 37 superior rooms available on ground floor, first floor and top floor. All rooms of Adi Dharma Cottages are with balcony facing to garden or to pool, with individually self controlled air-conditioning, refrigerator with mini bar, direct dial telephone, international television channel with in house movie program, hair dryer and hot shower over bath tub.

**ACCOMMODATION:**

 

ADI DHARMA HOTEL has 69 comfortably appointed superior rooms, 9 deluxe rooms, 4 super deluxe room and 5 family rooms

All rooms of ADI DHARMA HOTEL are with balcony facing to garden or to pool, with individually self controled air-conditioning, refrigerator with mini bar, direct dial telephone, international television channel with in house movie program, safety box, hair dryer and hot shower over bath tub. Coffee maker is also available in Super Deluxe Room

**Facilities**

**Adi Cempaka Restaurant & Bar** Many option of Western and Indonesia cuisine, tropical drinks to choose, good Bali coffee to chat the afternoon away, and a great cool beer to quench your thirst. And above it all, live music entertainment to keep you company as the sun goes down.

A place where boundaries do not exist. All around is a living room, each table a sight of diverse cultures. The fabulous food, friendly service and cool atmosphere makes it a memorable evening out.

Adi Cempaka Restaurant is open 24 hours caters for international tastes as well as offering Indonesian cuisine, Pool with swim up bar, sun deck to lounge open 7 am - 9 pm. Adi Dharma Hotel also has meditation hall, and culture activities such as Balinese dance and music. Instructor available on request.

 

**Swimming Pool**

A pool designed for romance and relaxation. It is just perfect for a glorious moonlight swim or sunbathing. If you have to choose between two option, choose both. After all, it's a holiday mean for two.

[![Adidharma\_exterior 2](/images/adi-dharma-hotel/Adidharma_exterior-2.jpg)](http://triaumatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/Adidharma_exterior-2.jpg)

 

**Restaurant**

Nasi Bali Restaurant is set conveniently between Legian street and Adi Dharma pool offers Authentic Balinese food, as well as international dishes. Open 7 am - 12 pm ( 7:00 hrs - 24:00 hrs ) Nasi Bali Restaurant is probably one of the best Balinese food restaurant in Legian street - Kuta, Bali Room service is available from 7 am to 11 pm Swimming pool with sun lounges

Femous Kuta Beach is 15 minutes walk away, a free shuttle bus is available to Legian shopping area and beach fiv e times a day as scheduled from both properties

[![Adidharma\_exterior 1](/images/adi-dharma-hotel/Adidharma_exterior-1.jpg)](http://triaumatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/Adidharma_exterior-1.jpg)

 

 

Nestled in the heart of Kuta, yet free the hustle bustle of the surrounding area, Adi Dharma Hotel is an oasis of greenery and tranquility to assure you of a comfortable stay. Short drive to and from the airport 10 minutes, from Sanur and Nusa Dua 15 and 25 minutes, from to the city of Denpasar.

 

Adi Dharma Hotel Jl. Benesari, Kuta, Bali, Indonesia
