A Balinese meal begins with ingredients, traditions, and stories shared across generations.

## Tasting island traditions

Explore local markets with a knowledgeable cook, visit small farms, and discover the depth behind dishes rooted in island produce, fragrant herbs, and spice. Seek intimate family kitchens and community-led dining experiences rather than generic cooking demonstrations.
