Travel inland to discover a different side of Bali, shaped by misty mountains, fertile valleys, and ancient pathways.

## Into the highlands

Thoughtful guides and village stays turn secluded landscapes into meaningful journeys. Start early for cooler temperatures, choose locally owned experiences, and leave enough time to enjoy each place without rushing.
