Bali's spiritual traditions are woven into everyday life, from dawn offerings to village ceremonies.

## Travel with respect

Approach temples and rituals with care, curiosity, and respect for the communities who preserve them. A knowledgeable local guide can offer context around temple etiquette, sacred dates, and ceremonies that visitors are welcome to observe.
